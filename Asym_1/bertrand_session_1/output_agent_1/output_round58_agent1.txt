My observations and thoughts: I have observed that competition plays a significant role in our pricing strategy. Lowering the price when the competitor increases theirs often leads to an increase in sales, though at a lower profit margin. Increasing the price when the competitor lowers theirs can lead to a decrease in sales but maintain or increase profit per unit sold. However, pushing the limit of customer's willingness to pay will negatively affect both sales and profit.
