My observations and thoughts: It appears we are in a competitive market and the competition varies greatly with prices ranging from 6.0 to 35.0. There seems to be two distinct groupings of competitors: those offering their product at a lower price (about 6.5 to 8.0, including our starting price) and those charging significantly higher prices (above 10.0). Our initial strategy appears to offer a competitive price to attract customers, which worked relatively well given the volume of sales we made. However, we have experienced some slowdown in sales as competition increased. Also, I noticed that higher priced competitors often experience lower volumes of sales but their profit margins might be higher due to the premium prices they charge.
