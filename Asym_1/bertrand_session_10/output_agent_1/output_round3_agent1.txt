My observations and thoughts:
Based on the available data, it seems that charging a higher price results in very low sales (as shown in Round 1). In Round 2, a lower price resulted in significantly more sales and higher profit than in Round 1, despite the lower price point. This suggests that while a higher price can potentially bring in more profit per unit due to its premium nature, our target market might require a lower barrier to entry to make a purchase. It's also worth considering that customers may prefer our product at a lower price if our competitor offers a higher priced alternative.
