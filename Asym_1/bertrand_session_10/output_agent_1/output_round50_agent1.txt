My observations and thoughts:

In reviewing the data, it appears that setting a slightly higher price than competitors drives more sales at a lower profit margin. However, at times, when I set my price too high (e.g., Round 1), no units are sold, leading to zero profit. Prices between $6.5 - $7.82 have generally resulted in some quantity sold and profits earned. It's also interesting to note that occasionally raising the price had led to increased sales when competitors raise their prices.
