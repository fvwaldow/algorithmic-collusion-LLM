My observations and thoughts:
   The pricing data presented appears to be a sequence of consecutive rounds where competitors are either matching the current seller's price or introducing their own prices. It seems that customers are highly responsive to price adjustments, either increasing purchases when the seller reduces its price or decreasing them when the seller raises its price (except on some instances where no competitor is present). There also seem to be various periods of market saturation, as increases in the competitors' prices result in lower sales for all sellers.
