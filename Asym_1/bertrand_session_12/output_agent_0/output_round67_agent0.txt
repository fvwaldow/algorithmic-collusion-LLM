My observations and thoughts:
This dataset provides a history of price and quantity sold for a product in competition with another product. It appears that the seller's initial prices were quite high compared to the competitor, but over time they lowered their price significantly in an attempt to increase sales. However, the lowering of the price did not seem to have a significant impact on the number of units sold as the numbers remained relatively low regardless of the price. One striking observation is that when the seller's price was comparable to or lower than the competitor, they saw a slight increase in quantity sold.
