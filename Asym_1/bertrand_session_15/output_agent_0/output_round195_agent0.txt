My observations and thoughts:
   It appears that there has been a significant fluctuation in both the competitor's price and my own price throughout this exercise. The competitor seems to have adopted a strategy of keeping their price relatively low initially, around $15-$20, then raising it to $20 or above for some rounds before dropping back down again.

   I have also noticed that when my price was set higher than the competitor ($25+), my quantity sold significantly dropped or even stopped, indicating a high sensitivity of customers to the price increase. However, when my price was lower or similar to the competitor, my quantity sold increased proportionally.

   In terms of insights, it seems that while setting a lower price initially can attract more customers and lead to higher sales quantities, doing so might not be profitable due to low profit margins. On the other hand, increasing the price when the competition drops theirs could potentially capitalize on this temporary situation; however, as seen in many rounds, an increase in price may cause a reduction in sales quantity and overall profit if it becomes too high compared to the competitor.
