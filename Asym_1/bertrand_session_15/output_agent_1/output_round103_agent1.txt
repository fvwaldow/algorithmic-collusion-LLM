My observations and thoughts: It is evident that there appears to be a consistent dynamic where setting prices significantly lower than the competition seems to increase sales volume dramatically. However, this strategy comes at a cost as profit margins are reduced due to the low price point. On the other hand, higher priced items rarely sell, even though they have higher profit margins. In short, balancing volume and profit is critical when determining pricing.
