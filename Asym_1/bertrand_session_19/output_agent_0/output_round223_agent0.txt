My observations and thoughts: In this data set, we see a price war occurring between our product and the competitor's product. There are instances where our price is lower than the competitor's, leading to a higher quantity of our product being sold. However, when our price is higher, the competitor seems to dominate the market. It appears that customers are more sensitive to price increases than decreases as there are fewer sales when the price goes up. We also notice that extremely high prices result in zero or very low sales.
