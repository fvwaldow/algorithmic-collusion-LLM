My observations and thoughts: It appears that adjusting the product price significantly higher or lower than the competitor does not seem always beneficial for profit, as the number of units sold decreases with a high price or increases with a low price at some expense to overall profit margin. The pricing strategy should be sensitive to the changes in the competitive environment and should aim to capitalize on moments when competitors' prices are either too high or too low.
