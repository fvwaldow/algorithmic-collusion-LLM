My observations and thoughts: The previous prices tested show that a slight price advantage (0.15 or less) over the competitor leads to increased profitability. However, going too high (as in round 7 at 3.25) results in very low quantity sold and therefore low profit. It looks like discount incentives increase both volume and profit, but should be used judiciously. The highest profit was earned when the price was closer to the customer's limit of $5.2 (round 11 at 2.85).
