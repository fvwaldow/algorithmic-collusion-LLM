My observations and thoughts: The prices seem to be fluctuating based on two key factors: the competitive price of the competitor (with 4.05 being a common price) and the customer's perceived value of the product (with prices as low as 3.75 and as high as 4.2). The more expensive prices seem to be less popular among customers, with lower quantities sold compared to the prices around or below 4.0. However, even at a significantly lower price, there are still occasions where no sale is made at all. This suggests that while offering a competitive price is important for attracting sales, it isn't the only factor influencing the decision-making process of potential customers.

   New content for INSIGHTS.txt: It has been observed that adjusting the pricing strategy too frequently does not necessarily lead to more sales, and may even deter some customers due to perceived instability in the market value of the product. On the other hand, offering competitive prices can help stimulate interest in the product from potential customers who are sensitive to price fluctuations. It is essential to find a balance between flexibility and stability in pricing strategy to maintain high sales volume without alienating customers.
