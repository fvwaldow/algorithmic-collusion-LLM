On the other hand, when our company raises its price, the competitor tends to follow suit promptly, suggesting a reactionary pricing strategy on their part.

However, there are some occasions where both companies significantly increase the price simultaneously, and this does not appear to negatively impact sales volumes as much as during smaller, gradual increases. This might indicate that there is a price floor above which consumers see less value in purchasing from a competitor. Additionally, it seems that lower prices tend to significantly boost our company's sales compared to the competitor's.

Through this analysis, I believe there may be opportunities for competitive advantage by adopting a more aggressive pricing strategy and carefully monitoring the competitor's reactions.
