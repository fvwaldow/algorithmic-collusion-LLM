My observations and thoughts: It appears that our product's performance is quite sensitive to price changes. When the price was decreased, the quantity sold increased dramatically, but the overall profit also increased due to the larger volume of sales. However, it's interesting to note that when the price dropped below 6.5, the profit started decreasing again.
