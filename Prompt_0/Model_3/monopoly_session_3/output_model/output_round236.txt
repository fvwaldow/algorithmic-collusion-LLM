New content for INSIGHTS.txt:
- It is crucial to understand price elasticity of demand - how much the quantity demanded changes with a change in price.
* Identify and capitalize on peak demand periods where higher prices can translate into larger profits.
* Offering occasional discounts or lowering prices during off-peak times could drive sales volume and potentially offset lower profit margins.
