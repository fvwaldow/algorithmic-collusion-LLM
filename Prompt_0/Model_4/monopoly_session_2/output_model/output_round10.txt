Consider expanding on tiered pricing by introducing discounts for orders of 5, 10, or 20 units. New content for INSIGHTS.txt:
Sales volumes dip sharply at higher price points, suggesting that the elasticity of demand is high within this range. Implementing tiered pricing could mitigate this effect by offering more attractive options to larger buyers without drastically reducing unit prices. The loyalty program insight proves valuable; it not only aids in retaining current customers but also encourages them to spend more incrementally over time. It's pivotal to track customer response rates to these strategies and be ready to adjust tactics if initial reactions are underwhelming.
