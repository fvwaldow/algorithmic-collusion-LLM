My observations and thoughts:
The data shows a fluctuating relationship between price points and quantity sold, with higher prices generally leading to lower quantities sold but potentially higher profit per item, while lower prices lead to increased sales volume but reduced profit per sale. The promotional strategy of offering limited-time discounts at $11.25 and $11.75 seems to have been effective in Round 19 and Round 16, achieving the highest profits per round. However, profit per round at these price points was not consistently high, indicating that either the promotional period was too short or other market factors influenced customer behavior. 

To maximize long-term profit, it would be beneficial to further analyze customer purchasing patterns and responses to promotions. Additionally, enhancing product value through storytelling and building loyalty programs could help justify higher prices, especially within the $11-$14.34 range, potentially leading to increased perceived value and willingness to pay.
