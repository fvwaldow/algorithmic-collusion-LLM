My observations and thoughts:

The data shows a large amount of variation in prices across different rounds. However, there are some patterns observed in the data that could be useful for determining an optimal price.

1.  The profit earned tends to increase with increases in price up to a certain point, but then decreases afterwards.
2.  At higher prices (above $5.10), there's a significant decrease in sales quantity, which results in lower total profits.
3.  Pricing between $4.90 and $5.00 seems to be the sweet spot for balancing profit earned and sales quantity.
