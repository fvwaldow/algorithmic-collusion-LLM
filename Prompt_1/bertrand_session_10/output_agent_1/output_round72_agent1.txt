My observations and thoughts:
It appears from this data that the relationship between price and quantity sold is not straightforward. Initially, as the price increased from 12.5 to 10.5 in Round 1, no product was sold (quantity sold = 0), suggesting an extremely high price point. In Round 2, a slight decrease in price to 10.5 resulted in a barely noticeable increase in quantity sold (0.59). However, as the price further decreased in Rounds 3 and 4, the quantities sold increased significantly. The largest jump was seen between Rounds 5 and 6 when the price dropped from 9.5 to 6.0, causing a substantial leap in quantities sold (from 1.96 to 46.69).
