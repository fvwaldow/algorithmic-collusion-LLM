In contrast, keeping the price high ensures fewer unit sales and a higher profit per sale, provided there is sufficient demand to cover production costs and make a profit. Price elasticity appears to be moderate; while demand does respond to changes in price, it is not highly sensitive.

In situations where the competitor increases their price, such as rounds 94 and 96, a decrease in our product's price can attract customers seeking cheaper alternatives. When the competitor maintains a lower price, however, as seen in rounds 92 and 93, increasing our own price or maintaining it at a competitive level might prove more profitable.
