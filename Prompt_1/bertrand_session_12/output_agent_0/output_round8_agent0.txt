My observations and thoughts: It appears that changing the price has had mixed results thus far. Higher prices have led to lower quantities sold but higher profit per unit, while lower prices have resulted in higher quantity sold but lower profit per unit. Customer perception of value seems to play a significant role in choosing our price due to the high willingness to pay a premium and the volatile competitor pricing trends. The data suggests that there is a sweet spot for pricing where both volume and profit can be maximized, however it may require more flexible strategies and further analysis of customer feedback to determine this point.
