My observations and thoughts:
I observed that lower prices tend to result in higher quantity sold but lower profit, while higher prices lead to lower volume of sales with higher profits. The price range where the product is slightly cheaper or equal to competitors leads to an increase in the quantity sold within reasonable profit margins. The data suggests that there might be a sweet spot between $5 and $6 for maximizing the number of units sold while still maintaining high profitability. In addition, it appears that frequent analysis of sales data could help identify this optimal pricing point more accurately.
