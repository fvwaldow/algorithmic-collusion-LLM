My observations and thoughts:
   Over the course of 21 pricing rounds, the main observation is that the market reacts strongly to changes in price. Lowering our price significantly helps boost sales volume but it also results in lower profit margins. Meanwhile, increasing the price reduces the number of units sold but raises the profit per unit, with the exception of instances where we set prices too high and risk losing significant market share. It appears that a sweet spot exists between $10 and $15 which enables us to maximize both sales volume and profit margin while remaining competitive.
