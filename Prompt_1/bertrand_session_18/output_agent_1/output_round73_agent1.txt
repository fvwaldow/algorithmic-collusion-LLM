My observations and thoughts: It appears that the prices fluctuate quite a bit based on the competitor's price. When our price is higher than the competitor's, we seem to sell more units, but at a loss. Conversely, when our price is lower than or equal to the competitor's, we sell fewer units but make a profit if the difference in prices is substantial enough. There also seems to be an inverse relationship between price and quantity sold - when the price goes up, the quantity goes down, and vice versa.

   - Adjust our own price dynamically in response to competitor's moves
   - Regularly review and analyze changes in sales quantity and profit margin

   New content for INSIGHTS.txt:
   - Lower prices tend to attract more customers but with smaller profits per unit sold

   - Staying competitive on price is essential for generating sales in this market
