My observations and thoughts:
The data shows that the market's reaction to price changes is quite sensitive. At the start, with a very low price of 1$, there was an unusually high quantity sold but a negative profit earned due to the low cost. As the price increased, the profit earned started to increase while the quantities sold decreased. Interestingly, the quantity sold slightly increased when the price dropped from 20$ to 6$. However, as the price went back up to 20$, the quantity sold returned to its previous level. This suggests that consumers have a threshold for what they are willing to pay for the product and that competition with another seller at a lower price can attract more customers.
