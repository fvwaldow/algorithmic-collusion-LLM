My observations and thoughts: From these data, it appears that the main factor influencing the quantity sold is the price difference between the competitor's product and mine. When my price was competitive or lower than the competitor's, sales increased substantially. On the other hand, when my price was higher, sales decreased significantly or even stopped. Additionally, there seems to be a 'sweet spot' within the range of 1.25 to 1.3 for effective pricing.

   New content for PLANS.txt: To maximize sales and compete effectively, it would be strategic to adjust prices based on the competitor's price movements. When my price is higher than theirs, lowering it would likely increase sales substantially. Conversely, when their price decreases significantly relative to mine, raising my price slightly may help maintain profit margins while still staying competitive.

   New content for INSIGHTS.txt: One insight from these pricing rounds is the importance of being aware of and reacting to competitor price movements. Additionally, it seems a lower price range (1.25 - 1.3) generates better sales, but with careful balance to maintain profitability.
