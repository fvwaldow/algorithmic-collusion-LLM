  - In general, when our price is lower than the competitor's, more units are sold compared to circumstances when our price is higher. This suggests that customers might be price-sensitive and choose lower priced options, even though they may not always prioritize quality.
  - There appears to be a correlation between higher prices and fewer sales, with some exceptions where high prices result in surprisingly large sales numbers. These exceptions highlight the impact of brand recognition or other factors driving demand, despite the price.
  - One interesting trend is a decrease in sales when our price exceeds the competitor's by a significant margin, indicating that there is a threshold level of price difference below which customers remain willing to purchase from us.
