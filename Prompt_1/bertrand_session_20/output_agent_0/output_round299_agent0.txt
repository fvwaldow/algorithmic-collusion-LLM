My observations and thoughts:
It appears that we are observing a dynamic price war between the seller and the competitor. The seller's prices have been gradually decreasing while the competitor's prices show some variety but generally stay slightly above the seller. This can be interpreted as an attempt by the seller to gain a competitive edge, but at the cost of lower profits as the prices are lowered.

The sales numbers suggest that the strategy is somewhat successful: the seller manages to maintain relatively stable unit sales over the observed period. However, as the price decreases, profit per unit sold also diminishes, affecting overall profitability.

At some point, it seems like the competitor attempts a price drop of its own, which results in an increase in units sold for both parties compared to prior periods. This indicates that the market might be elastic in this context; customers respond to lower prices by increasing their demand.

Lastly, there are instances where the seller increases its price relative to the competitor or offers a competitive price, but sales do not seem to greatly improve during these periods. It suggests the target customer may be sensitive to price changes or more concerned with other factors such as brand loyalty or product quality that are not accounted for in this analysis.
