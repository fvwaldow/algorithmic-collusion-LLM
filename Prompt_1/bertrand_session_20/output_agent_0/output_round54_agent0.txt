My observations and thoughts: It appears that the optimal pricing strategy varies greatly depending on the competitor's price. In contrast, when the competitor prices low (below 30), the product performs better at higher prices (above 30). The product fares worst against very high competitor prices (45.0) and extremely low ones (27.5). It seems beneficial to monitor the competitor's price strategy closely to react accordingly.
