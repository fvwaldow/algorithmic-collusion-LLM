- When the competitor prices their product higher than mine, they tend to sell fewer units and I maintain profitability. This suggests a price-sensitivity among customers, where higher prices limit sales despite offering substantial markups.

- However, when they lower their prices to match or undercut my own, sales volume greatly increases. Customers seem more willing to purchase when competitors offer competitive pricing.

- It's important to note that in many cases I can still outperform the competition by maintaining a slightly higher price point and ensuring product quality. This strategy requires careful control of costs and constant quality improvement to justify the premium price.
