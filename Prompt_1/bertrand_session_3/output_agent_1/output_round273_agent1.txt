My observations and thoughts:
  I observed fluctuations in both my sales quantity and profit earned during this pricing experiment. At lower prices (1.85, 1.95, 1.98), the profit earned per unit is less than at higher prices (2.0, 2.05, 3.0), but the total number of units sold was significantly higher. This suggests that setting a competitive price attracts more customers, but each sale may not be as profitable. Conversely, higher prices result in fewer sales yet generate greater profit per sale.

   The higher priced items (2.35 and 3.0) had the lowest total sales, suggesting that customers are not willing to pay such high prices for this product. However, these high-priced items also provided significant profits when sold due to their high price points.

   Another interesting observation is the lack of an impact from changing the competitor's price on my own sales and profit. This could suggest the market is saturated with similar products or that customers are not particularly sensitive to small variations in competitor prices.
