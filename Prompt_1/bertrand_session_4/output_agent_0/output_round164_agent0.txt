My observations and thoughts:
It appears that the market is sensitive to pricing. When the price was higher than competitors (e.g., $3.5 or $10), sales were minimal ($0 or a few units). Interestingly, several rounds saw extreme sales when the price was as low as $1.00, although these incidents are quite rare and may not be feasible for long-term sustainability. It seems that finding a sweet spot between competitive prices and unique offers could result in improved profits.
