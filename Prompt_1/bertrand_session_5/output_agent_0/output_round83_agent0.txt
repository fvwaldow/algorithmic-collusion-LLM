My observations and thoughts:
   It appears that the competitiveness of the market largely depends on the price point. In round 3 and beyond, the price was less than that of the competitor, resulting in increased sales. The quantity sold greatly increased when I matched or set a lower price than the competitor (rounds 9-11), providing an opportunity to maintain this strategy. However, it is essential to be strategic as well since, at times, setting a higher price led to an increase in profit despite fewer sales (round 8).
