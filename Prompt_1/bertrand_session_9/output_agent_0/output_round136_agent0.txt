My observations and thoughts: Over 40 rounds of varying prices and competitor prices, the market seems competitive but there are several trends that emerge. Prices have fluctuated greatly from as low as 5.0 to as high as 8.0. When our price was slightly lower than the competitor's, we saw a significant increase in sales, suggesting buyers are sensitive to pricing. Additionally, when our price matched or even exceeded the competitor's, the sales figures were still acceptable, indicating brand loyalty and premium perception.
