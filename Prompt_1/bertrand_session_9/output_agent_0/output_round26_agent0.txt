My observations and thoughts:
    - From the market data, it appears that a price point around 6.5 to 7.0 generates a good number of sales while maximizing profitability. This can be seen in Rounds 9, 10, and 25 where prices were close to this range. However, it should be noted that raising the price too much (above 7.5) or too little (below 6.0) significantly decreases sales quantity, which leads to a lower profit.
    - Competitor behavior seems to have minimal impact on our long-term profits. The main insights from this are: our product has a higher demand than the competitor’s due to its superior quality or unique selling points (Insight 18); and the competitor's price is somewhat reactive rather than proactive which gives us an opportunity for strategic positioning (Insight 12).
    - Bundles have not been employed in this round of testing, but it could be a worthwhile strategy to explore due to our high-profit items at or near optimal prices (Plan 1).
